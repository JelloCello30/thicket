Built like it's watching your browser. Because it is.

A tab manager sees a lot. So the boring privacy work came first, and it's not fine print — it's architecture.

Read the privacy policy →

  • Organization happens on your device

    Grouping runs locally in the extension. Signed out, nothing ever leaves your browser.

  • There is no account, and no server

    Nothing is uploaded: the extension ships without the code to make a network request. Grouping, search, summaries and comparisons all run in your browser.

  • Banking and health sites are excluded automatically

    A built-in list keeps sensitive sites out of everything. Add your own exclusions — company tools, anything — in two clicks.

  • Incognito is invisible. Always.

    Private windows are never observed, never recorded, no setting required.

  • Export or delete everything, for real

    Settings has two buttons: download everything Thicket holds as JSON, or erase all of it. No request to file — nobody else has a copy.

Free, and it stays on your machine.
